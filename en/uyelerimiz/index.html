Board of Directors:

Our board of directors, consisting of 15 principal and 15 alternate members, represents the association, manages the budget and implements strategic decisions.

Audit Board:

Our audit board, consisting of 3 principal and 3 alternate members, audits the association's financial and administrative transactions and prepares reports.

Membership Types:

Regular Member:

Takes part in the association's main activities and enjoys all rights. Has the right to vote and be elected.

Honorary Member:

Honorary membership is granted to persons who provide material or moral contributions to the association and support the association's purposes.
