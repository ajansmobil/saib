Syria and UAE discuss to increase finance, banking and trade cooperation

ABU DHABI (SANA) – UAE Vice President and Minister of Presidential Affairs Sheikh Mansour bin Zayed Al Nahyan discussed ways to increase bilateral cooperation in various fields, especially finance, banking and trade, between the UAE and Syria with Syrian Finance Minister Mohammed Yasser Berniyye and Central Bank of Syria Governor Abdulkadir Husriye.

At the meeting held at Al Sani Palace in Abu Dhabi in May 2025, the parties discussed mechanisms to advance the common interests of the two countries. UAE Minister Mansour bin Zayed emphasized the UAE's willingness to support stability and economic-administrative reforms in Syria. Syrian Finance Minister Berniyye stated that this contact was important in terms of strengthening relations between the two brotherly countries and drew attention to the UAE side's determined support for Syria's economic recovery. The meeting also discussed issues such as investment opportunities in the finance and banking sector, energy and infrastructure projects, tax system reform and strengthening of the judicial structure. This high-level contact is evaluated as part of the economic diplomacy traffic developing between the UAE and Syria in the post-2011 period. Indeed, immediately after the meeting, the UAE announced that it would provide support to Syria in areas such as electricity grid repair and infrastructure financing, and joint working groups were established between the two countries in the banking field. This constructive attitude of the UAE towards Syria was an important development that accelerated Syria's economic integration with the Arab world.

At least half of Syrians will return

After the fall of the Baath regime, Syrians began to return to their country. Syrian Businessmen Association President Halit Savafoğlu said, "We think that at least half of the Syrians living in Turkey will return. Those who see that their country has improved will go back.
