Comprehensive cooperation agreement between Syria and Turkey postal administrations

DUBAI (SANA) – Syrian Post Authority Director General Imadeddin Hamd signed a memorandum of understanding with Turkey Post and Telegraph Organization (PTT) Director General Hakan Gülten to strengthen bilateral cooperation at an event held in the UAE as part of the 28th Universal Postal Union Congress.

According to the Syrian Post Authority's statement, the signed memorandum of understanding creates a comprehensive cooperation framework between the two institutions. Integration of platforms and facilitation of product shopping are particularly targeted in the fields of cargo and e-commerce. With this step, it is expected that the quality of services offered to customers in both countries will increase. The agreement also covers issues such as technical and administrative experience sharing, development of logistics infrastructure, improvement of distribution channels, and use of common facilities in import-export processes. While the Syrian Post Authority evaluates this initiative as an important step towards expanding cooperation at the international level, it aims to strengthen the institution's presence in the logistics and electronic services market and offer more effective solutions to citizens both inside and outside Syria. Experts state that this agreement in the postal field will also contribute to commercial integration between the two countries, and that cross-border cargo and e-commerce transactions will accelerate. In this context, joint operation in postal services between Syria and Turkey will facilitate regional trade and offer new opportunities especially to small and medium-sized e-commerce businesses.
