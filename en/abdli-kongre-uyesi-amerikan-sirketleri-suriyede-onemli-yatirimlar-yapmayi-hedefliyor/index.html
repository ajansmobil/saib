US Congressman: American companies aim to make significant investments in Syria

WASHINGTON (SANA) – US House of Representatives member Marlin Stutzman stated that American companies aim to make large and significant investments in infrastructure, energy and banking sectors in Syria.

Congressman Stutzman drew attention to the US business world's interest in Syria by saying in his post on social media platform X that "Free Syria can be a haven for American companies' investments in infrastructure, energy and banking sectors." Stutzman, who was part of the US Congressional delegation that visited Syria, also emphasized in his post-visit statements that there are many opportunities for cooperation with the Syrian government and that many American business people are eager to make large investments in Syria. Stutzman expressed that it is also important to preserve the country's rich religious and cultural diversity for Syria's reconstruction and future success. Experts state that with the easing of sanctions in the US, American energy and construction companies may prepare to enter the Syrian market, and that this is critical for Syria's reintegration into the global financial system. Stutzman's message was evaluated as a sign that American companies are willing to evaluate investment opportunities in Syria after many years.

Trade Ministry: Export and transit restrictions to Syria lifted

Turkish Trade Ministry lifted restriction lists on exports and transit to Syria with circular 2025/4. Imports from Syria were also normalized; transit of goods from Syria to third countries via Turkey was liberalized except for metal scrap. The measure entered into force as part of the implementation of the two countries' agreement following the regime change of 8 December 2024.

Syrian President Ahmed Shara: We are determined for strategic partnership with Turkey

Syrian President Ahmed Shara stated that they are determined to elevate relations with Turkey to the level of strategic partnership. He expressed that they have launched joint cooperation in humanitarian, economic, cultural and social fields.

President Erdoğan hosts Syrian President Ahmed Shara in Ankara

Syrian Transition President Ahmed Shara visited Turkey for the first time on 4 February 2025. A meeting of approximately 3 hours was held at the Presidential Complex upon President Erdoğan's invitation. Erdoğan emphasized Syria's territorial integrity; pledged support to Syria in the fight against ISIS and PKK. Shara proposed deep strategic cooperation and cooperation in humanitarian and economic fields, and invited Erdoğan to Damascus.

Turkey-Syria Economic Cooperation Summit held in Gaziantep

The Turkey-Syria Economic Cooperation Summit was held at Gaziantep Şahinbey Congress Center on 4 February 2025 by MÜSİAD and IBF. Trade Minister Ömer Bolat, Gaziantep Governor and Mayor attended. Approximately 700 Turkish and 500 Syrian businesspeople participated. It was announced that Yayladağı border gate was opened for humanitarian passage, major cuts were made in customs duties, and negotiations for a Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement would begin. 107 companies set up B2B tables.

Turkey's exports to Syria increased by 36.7% in 2025

Turkey's exports to Syria in the first 4 months of 2025 increased by 36.7% compared to the same period last year, rising from 489 million 837 thousand dollars to 669 million 661 thousand dollars. In April, exports were 162 million 109 thousand dollars with a 57.6% increase. 1.5 billion dollars in exports is expected in the first half of 2025. The revival started after the regime change of 8 December 2024.

Turkey-Syria defence and military cooperation talks continue

Developing military and security cooperation between Turkey and Syria is on the agenda. A high-level delegation from the Ministry of National Defence made a technical visit to Damascus on 29-30 January. A joint roadmap is envisaged for enhancing the capacity of the Syrian army. A possible defence agreement was discussed during Ahmed Shara's visit to Ankara on 4 February.
