Mining exports rise 19.8 percent in ten months to $6.1 billion

Mining product exports grew 19.8 percent year on year in January-October to $6.099 billion (6,098,835 thousand dollars), with shipments to 209 countries.

According to figures compiled by Anadolu Agency from the Turkish Exporters Assembly (TIM), mining product exports rose 19.8 percent in January-October compared with the same period last year, reaching $6,098,835 thousand. Over the same period Türkiye's total exports grew 5.5 percent to $236.8 billion, and mining products accounted for 2.6 percent of the total.

The sector sold to 209 countries. Exports to China, the largest market, rose 47.8 percent to $1.919 billion and made up about 31.5 percent of the sector's exports on their own. China was followed by the United States with $490 million, Bulgaria with $414 million, Spain with $293 million and India with $262 million.

By province, Istanbul came first with $1.720 billion, followed by Ankara with $1.327 billion, Elazig with $336 million, Denizli with $267 million and Izmir with $266 million. Although monthly figures fluctuated, exports were above their 2025 levels in every month except May.

Source: Anadolu Agency — 10 November 2026
