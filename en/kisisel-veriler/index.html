Protection of Personal Data

As SAIB Association, we collect personal data of our members and visitors only for the purposes of conducting association activities, meeting legal requirements, processing membership requests and providing association services. The security of your personal data is a priority for us, and we take the necessary technical and administrative measures to protect this data within the framework of all relevant legislation, primarily Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK).

The collected personal data is accessible only by the association management and authorized persons, and is not shared with third parties without your consent. Our members and visitors have the right to learn for what purposes their personal data is processed, how it is stored, and with whom it is shared. In addition, you can request access to your data, request its correction, deletion or anonymization.

If you have any questions or requests regarding your personal data, you can contact our association headquarters. The security and privacy of our members' and visitors' data is of great importance to SAIB Association.
