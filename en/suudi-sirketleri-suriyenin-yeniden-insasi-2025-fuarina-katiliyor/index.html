Saudi companies participate in "Syria's Reconstruction 2025" fair

The "Reconstruction Syria 2025" fair held in Damascus witnessed intensive participation from Saudi companies specialized in construction, building materials and interior design. The event aims to strengthen economic cooperation between the two countries and increase investment and reconstruction opportunities in Syria.

The 'Syria's Reconstruction 2025' fair that started in October 2025 at the Damascus International Fairground was attended by 260 companies from 23 countries. Leading companies from Saudi Arabia opened stands and introduced their products and projects. Taif Nejdi Group, with 47 years of history, participated in the fair with metal roof covering and paint products, while company officials emphasized that Syria is a promising market for investment. TDC Construction company showcased its experience in strategic projects carried out in the Middle East, and its representative Fahd El Maliki stated that they believe Syria will achieve economic and infrastructure growth in a short time. While 'Unwan Decor' company presented local wood products, its manager expressed that close cooperation among Arab companies is essential for the success of major reconstruction projects. Riyadh Ceramic Factory introduced its capacity to produce 100,000 square meters of high-quality ceramic tiles per day and reiterated Saudi Arabia's support for Syria's reconstruction projects. Additionally, Al Dar wallpaper company, which has been operating since 1936, announced that it offers training programs to young Syrian talents. Saudi company representatives at the fair stated that they aim to contribute to knowledge transfer and production capacity development by working together with Syrian institutions. The three-day fair served as a regional platform for the reconstruction of Syria's infrastructure and production sectors, and at the end of the fair, many Saudi investors expressed their intention to invest in concrete projects in Syria.
