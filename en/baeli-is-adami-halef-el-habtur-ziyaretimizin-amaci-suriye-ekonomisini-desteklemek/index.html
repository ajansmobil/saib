UAE businessman Khalaf Al-Habtoor: The purpose of our visit is to support the Syrian economy

DAMASCUS (SANA) – Businessman Khalaf Al-Habtoor from the United Arab Emirates today began a working visit to Syria to discuss a series of economic issues with relevant officials in Syria.

Khalaf Al-Habtoor, Chairman of the Board of Dubai-based Al Habtoor Group, came to Damascus in October 2025 and discussed economic cooperation opportunities with Syrian officials. Al-Habtoor, who was welcomed at Damascus Airport by the President of the Syrian Investment Authority, said in his statement to SANA that the purpose of his visit was to create job opportunities for Syrian youth and achieve concrete results that would support investment and production. The famous businessman, emphasizing that the UAE has always been with the Syrian people, expressed that there are many project ideas they can cooperate with Syrians and that these will turn into real partnerships in the future. Al-Habtoor stated that a new page focused on the economy has now opened in Syria, saying "I expect Syria to become one of the most important economic countries in the region within a few years. Syrians have an innate superior commercial intelligence." The UAE businessman praised the productivity and creativity of the Syrian people and stated that the most important area to focus on in relations between the two countries is economic cooperation. During this visit, various initiatives regarding tourism, infrastructure and investment projects were discussed, and the Al-Habtoor group examined potential investment areas in Syria on site. The visit was evaluated as a strong message that Gulf capital's participation in Syria's reconstruction process will increase.
