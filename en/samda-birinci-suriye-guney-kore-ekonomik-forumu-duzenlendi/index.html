First Syria-South Korea economic forum held in Damascus

The First Syria-Korea Economic Forum held in Damascus addressed ways to develop economic and investment partnerships between the two countries and increase cooperation in the fields of development and reconstruction.

The Syria-South Korea Economic Forum held in Damascus in December 2025 was attended by Jisoo Pyo, Director of the Middle East Department of the South Korean Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and officials from the Korea Trade-Investment Promotion Agency (KOTRA). The Korean side emphasized infrastructure projects, trade in food and cosmetic products, and acceleration of customs procedures as priority areas that could support Syria's economic recovery. KOTRA representative Jae Won Joo highlighted joint investment opportunities in sectors such as information technologies, smart cities, transportation, construction equipment, renewable energy, medicine and pharmaceuticals. At the forum, it was stated that South Korea proposed a gradual cooperation strategy; a plan starting with basic humanitarian and medical needs, then moving to reconstruction equipment in the short term, then to comprehensive infrastructure projects with a public-private partnership model, and finally to long-term investments. Steps such as investment protection agreements, prevention of double taxation, and opening of financial channels were also discussed to improve the investment environment. Syrian officials expressed that the entry of South Korean companies into the country is an important opportunity for Syria's reintegration into the regional and global economy. It was emphasized that cooperation with Korea, especially in the automotive sector, technology transfer and development of local production, would accelerate Syrian industry. While the forum was evaluated as part of the Syrian government's policy of opening cooperation channels with countries with advanced technology and industrial capacity, the will for joint work on concrete projects between the two countries was demonstrated.
