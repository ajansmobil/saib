Dear SAIB Members and Valued Friends;

In the period of oppression that began approximately 12 years ago, we lost many lives in Syria, we witnessed war, we went hungry and thirsty. Yet we never gave up our cause. We fought for our future and our children. By the grace of God, at the end of 2024 we regained our freedom under the leadership of our President Ahmed El-Şara. Now is the time to stand up and grow with new goals.

Our Turkish state embraced and hosted the Syrian people for 12 years. In this regard, I extend my heartfelt thanks to you, the business people gathered here, and to the people of Gaziantep and Turkey.

While we experience unity and solidarity in this association, we will also engage in trade. In this context, we will do our utmost to support you. Under the leadership of our Turkish state, we are planning business partnerships with the Syrian state.

As you have been closely following, in Syria, many cities—especially Aleppo and Damascus—have begun to rise again. Wounds are being healed, life is returning to normal, and a new recovery process is advancing rapidly in the region.

We, as the SAIB Association, will carry out important work to take part in this reconstruction process, contribute to Syria's future, and above all to build a strong commercial bridge between Turkey and Syria.

I extend my respects to you all.
