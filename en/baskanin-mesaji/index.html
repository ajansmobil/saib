Dear Members and Business Partners,

As SAIB - International Syrian Businessmen Association, we set out with our mission to build strong economic bridges between Turkey and Syria and contribute to regional development. Our association works to facilitate commercial activities between the two countries, create new cooperation opportunities, and add value to our members.

In the future, we will continue to grow by bringing together more business people, developing joint projects, and creating a strong network in international markets. Your support and participation are the cornerstone of our success.

Respectfully,

Mehmet Abdi

SAIB President
