Export Guide

SAIB Export Support Programs

Our association offers comprehensive support programs to enable our members to gain competitive strength in international markets and increase their export potential.

Target Markets:

- Europe: EU countries, Balkans

- Middle East: Gulf countries, Syria, Iraq, Lebanon

- Africa: North African countries

Our Services:

1. Export Consulting

- Market research and analysis

- Target market identification

- Product suitability assessment

2. Business Development Support

- Finding potential buyers

- B2B meeting organization

- Commercial agreement process management

3. Training and Seminars

- Export legislation training

- Customs procedures and logistics

- International payment systems

4. Fairs and Events

- Support for participation in international fairs

- Business forums and networking events

- Trade delegation organization

Contact:

You can contact us for detailed information about our export consulting services.
