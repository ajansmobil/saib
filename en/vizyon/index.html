Our Vision

SAIB's vision is to create a strong network of economic cooperation in international markets by establishing permanent commercial bridges between Turkey and Syria. We aim to be a reliable and effective non-governmental organization that plays a leading role in regional and global trade and is the first choice of business people from both countries.

We want to be a dynamic platform where business people, industrialists and entrepreneurs can come together to maximize their commercial potential, develop innovative business models and easily access international markets. We see being in a leading position in the development of Turkey-Syria economic relations, leading the acceleration of regional development and contributing to sustainable economic growth as the foundation of our vision.
