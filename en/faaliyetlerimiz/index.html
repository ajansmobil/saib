SAIB Our Activities

ECONOMIC AND COMMERCIAL ACTIVITIES

- Development of trade between Syria and Turkey

- Creation of joint investment projects

- Organization of international fairs, seminars and business forums

EDUCATION AND CONSULTING

- Seminars, courses, panels and conferences are organized for members

- Consulting and business development support is provided to entrepreneurs

- Professional development programs are offered

SOCIAL AND CULTURAL ACTIVITIES

- Dinner meetings to increase solidarity among members

- Cultural events, trips, concerts and exhibitions are organized

- The association actively supports social responsibility projects

INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS COLLABORATIONS

- Joint projects are carried out with associations, foundations and commercial organizations abroad

- Export support programs are implemented in European, Middle Eastern and African markets

- Strategic business collaborations with chambers of commerce and exporters' associations

SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY WORK

- Aid projects for needy individuals and institutions

- Solidarity campaigns during earthquake, disaster and crisis periods

- Education and employment-focused social initiatives
