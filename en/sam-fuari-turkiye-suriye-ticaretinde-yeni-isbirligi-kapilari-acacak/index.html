Damascus Fair will open new cooperation doors in Turkey-Syria trade

Trade Minister Ömer Bolat stated that the 62nd Damascus International Fair, which will open its doors tomorrow in Syria, is of great importance in terms of reviewing the mutual cooperation of the two countries, especially taking international steps in the customs field.

Bolat stated that a large delegation of Turkish business people will participate in the Damascus Fair and that the fair will last until September 5. Expressing that Turkey's participation in this organization will contribute to the establishment of peace and stability and the strengthening of constructive dialogue, Bolat emphasized that the fair will concretize Turkey's support for Syria's reconstruction. Saying that there was strong participation from many sectors from food to construction, Bolat noted that steps towards trade liberalization between Turkey and Syria could be taken in the future, and that new agreements were signed with high-level contacts on the principle of 'win-win'.

At least half of Syrians will return

After the fall of the Baath regime, Syrians began to return to their country. Syrian Businessmen Association President Halit Savafoğlu said, "We think that at least half of the Syrians living in Turkey will return. Those who see that their country has improved will go back. However, we cannot completely break our ties. We have a debt of gratitude to this country. We ate bread here. We are grateful to Turkey."

Syrian economy growing faster than expected, sanctions easing

Central Bank of Syria Governor Abdulkadir Husriye announced that with the return of refugees and the end of the 14-year civil war, the economy grew much faster in 2025 than the 1% growth rate predicted by the World Bank.

Turkey's exports to Syria increased by 54%, exceeding 3 billion dollars

Turkey's exports to Syria increased by 54.1% in 2025 compared to the previous year, exceeding 3 billion dollars. This increase was achieved through the normalization of relations between the two countries and the strengthening of political stability in Syria.
