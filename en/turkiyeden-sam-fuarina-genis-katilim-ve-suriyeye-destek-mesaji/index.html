Wide participation from Turkey to Damascus Fair and support message to Syria

Turkish Trade Minister Ömer Bolat emphasized that his country's participation in the 62nd Damascus International Fair demonstrates Turkey's great importance to the stability of Syria and the region.

In his statement to the Syrian Arab News Agency (SANA), Bolat stated that the number of Turkish businesspeople participating in the fair reaching 500 is an indicator of Turkey's importance to developing relations between the two countries and increasing cooperation in all areas. Expressing that the Turkish government sees supporting Syria's economic growth and stability as a primary goal, Bolat said that Turkey has stood by the Syrian people in the past and will continue to meet Syria's needs in areas such as trade, services, energy, and defense. Emphasizing that Syria's unity and stability are of critical importance for the region, Bolat noted that Syria can regain its former strength with its existing capacity, and that relations are progressing with determined steps through the joint work of the two countries. Reminding that more than 800 local and foreign companies from 20 different countries participated in the 62nd Damascus Fair opened on August 28, and that the event took place in an area of 95,000 square meters, Bolat stated that the fair attracts thousands of visitors every day and witnessed the Syrian economy meeting with the world after a long break. While leading companies from many sectors exhibited their products and services in the Turkish pavilion, Turkey's strong participation sent a message to Syrians that they are with them in the normalization process.

After the fall of the Baath regime, Syrians began to return to their country.
