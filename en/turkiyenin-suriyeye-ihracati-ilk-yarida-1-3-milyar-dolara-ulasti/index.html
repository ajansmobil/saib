Türkiye's exports to Syria reach $1.3 billion in the first half, up 26.4 percent

According to EnterpriseAM, Türkiye's exports to Syria grew 26.4 percent year on year to $1.3 billion in the first half of 2026; the analysis highlights a shift in the relationship from trade towards investment and production.

According to EnterpriseAM's analysis of 19 October 2026, Türkiye's exports to Syria grew 26.4 percent year on year in the first half of 2026 to reach $1.3 billion, while Syrian exports to Türkiye stood at around $450 million. Total trade between the two countries rose 16 percent in the first seven months of 2026, after a 45 percent jump last year that peaked at $3.7 billion.

The analysis quotes Fuat Ozcorekci, a former co-chairman of the Turkey-Syria Business Council, who says the relationship has been mainly trade-driven and should increasingly become investment- and production-driven. In the model he describes, Gulf capital provides scale, Turkish companies bring engineering and project execution, and Syrian partners bring market access and labour.

On the Syrian side, work is also under way to improve the trade balance: the Syrian Exporters Board, under the supervision of Economy and Industry Minister Nidal Al Shaar, is identifying products that can achieve a balance in exports to Türkiye, starting with raw and semi-manufactured materials from the textile and food sectors. The board is also working on rehabilitating ports and border crossings and on an electronic system to ease exports.

Source: EnterpriseAM — 19 October 2026
