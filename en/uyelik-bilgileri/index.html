SAIB Membership

Membership in SAIB is open to all business people who want to benefit from commercial opportunities between Turkey and Syria, expand their business network and contribute to regional economic developments. Our association offers two different membership categories: individual and corporate membership.

Membership Conditions

• Natural and legal persons who carry out or plan to carry out commercial activities in the Republic of Turkey or Syria can become members.

• Membership applications are evaluated and approved by the association's board of directors.

• Members are subject to the rights and obligations specified in the association's charter.

• Annual membership fee is determined by the board of directors decision.

Membership Benefits

• B2B events and networking opportunities

• Export-import consulting and commercial information sharing

• Support for participation in sectoral fairs

• Being informed about investment and incentive programs

• Priority access to training seminars and workshops

• Cooperation opportunities with public and private sectors

You can contact our association headquarters for membership application or fill out the application form through our website.
