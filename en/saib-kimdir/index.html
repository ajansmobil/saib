About SAIB

SAIB (International Syrian Businessmen Association) is an international non-governmental organization established in Gaziantep to bring together business people from Turkey and Syria, facilitate commercial activities, create new commercial areas, and support economic development. Our association aims to develop regional and international trade by establishing permanent economic bridges between the two countries.

Our association creates commercial cooperation opportunities by bringing together industrialists, entrepreneurs and business people from Syria and Turkey in line with their common interests. We offer various services to our members such as export-import consulting, B2B (Business to Business) events, investment incentives, sectoral fairs and training programs. In addition, we contribute to regional development projects by acting as a bridge between the public and private sectors.

As SAIB, we have adopted the mission of increasing production, investment and export potential, uniting scientists and business people on a common platform, and accelerating regional economic development. By acting with the principles of transparency, reliability and cooperation, we aim to contribute to the success of our members and business partners. Our association, based in Gaziantep, plays a leading role in the development of Turkey-Syria economic relations.
