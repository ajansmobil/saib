Our Quality Policy

For our company, quality is not merely a result but a way of working built into every stage of the job. We regard customer satisfaction, compliance with legal requirements and continuous improvement as the foundation of our understanding of quality.

Our core principles

Our quality policy is based on the following principles:

  • Understanding customer expectations correctly and offering products and services that meet them
  • Carrying out our activities in accordance with the relevant legislation and the obligations we have undertaken
  • Making our processes defined, traceable and measurable
  • Applying controls aimed at preventing errors at their source
  • Supporting the training and participation of our employees

Continuous improvement

We regularly review customer feedback, our internal assessments and our process data, and identify opportunities for improvement. We implement the improvements identified in a planned manner and monitor their results.

Responsibility and sharing

Quality is the shared responsibility of every employee in our company. We also aim to share the same understanding with our suppliers and business partners, to state our expectations clearly and to develop together.

Your feedback is valuable: All your views and suggestions about our products and services are an important input to our quality work.
