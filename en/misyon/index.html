Our Mission

SAIB's mission is to contribute to regional development by developing economic cooperation between Turkey and Syria. Our main goal is to increase production, investment and export potential; to unite industrialists, scientists and business people in a common organization, and to support sustainable economic growth.

We add value to our members by providing consulting services that will facilitate their commercial activities, creating new commercial areas and cooperation opportunities, organizing B2B events and fairs, providing investment incentives and organizing training programs. At the same time, we support regional projects by acting as a bridge between the public and private sectors.

SAIB aims to contribute to the strengthening of Turkey-Syria economic relations and the welfare of business people in both countries by acting with the principles of transparency, reliability, mutual respect and cooperation. Among our forward-looking goals are reaching more members, expanding the international network and accelerating regional economic integration.
