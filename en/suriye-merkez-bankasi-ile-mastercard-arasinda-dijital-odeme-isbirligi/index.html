Digital payment cooperation between Central Bank of Syria and Mastercard

DAMASCUS (SANA) – The Central Bank of Syria announced that it signed a memorandum of understanding with Mastercard company to develop the infrastructure of digital payment systems in the country, share experience and increase financial inclusion.

According to the statement made by the Central Bank in September 2025, this strategic partnership aims to expand millions of people's access to basic financial services. The agreement reflects a common vision to strengthen financial inclusion and equal opportunity through technology. Adam Jones, President of Mastercard responsible for Western Arab Regions, stated that the agreement demonstrates both parties' belief in building a system based on financial inclusion and said "We want to offer the opportunities of the global payment world to the Syrian people, to integrate the latest financial innovations into the local service infrastructure." Thanks to this cooperation, the development of Syria's digital payment ecosystem, citizens and businesses gaining access to faster and more secure transactions, and the acceleration of the process of reintegration into international payment networks are expected.

At least half of Syrians will return

After the fall of the Baath regime, Syrians began to return to their country. Syrian Businessmen Association President Halit Savafoğlu said, "We think that at least half of the Syrians living in Turkey will return. Those who see that their country has improved will go back. However, we cannot completely break our ties. We have a debt of gratitude to this country. We ate bread here. We are grateful to Turkey."

Syrian economy growing faster than expected, sanctions easing

Central Bank of Syria Governor Abdulkadir Husriye announced that with the return of refugees and the end of the 14-year civil war, the economy grew much faster in 2025 than the 1% growth rate predicted by the World Bank.
