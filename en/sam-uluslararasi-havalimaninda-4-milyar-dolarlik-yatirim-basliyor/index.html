4 billion dollar investment begins at Damascus International Airport

The 4 billion dollar rehabilitation and capacity increase project of Damascus International Airport, to be carried out by a consortium including Kalyon Construction and Cengiz Construction, has begun.

With the investment and concession contracts signed on November 27, 2025, the modernization of Damascus International Airport officially entered the implementation phase. The consortium, which includes Kalyon and Cengiz Construction from Turkey, UCC from Qatar, and Assets Investments from the USA, will rehabilitate existing airport facilities and gradually improve operational quality by constructing new terminals, aprons and support structures. At the end of this large 4-phase project, the airport's annual passenger capacity is targeted to reach 31 million within 10 years (6 million by the end of 2026, 16 million with subsequent phases, and finally 31 million passengers/year). More than 90,000 direct and indirect jobs are expected to be created during the construction process. While Syria's first white cement factory is also laying its foundation in the Adra Industrial Zone as part of the project, it was announced that Saudi Arabia and other partners will establish three new cement factories in the country with an investment of approximately 11 billion Saudi Riyals. The Damascus Airport project aims to revive Syria's aviation infrastructure, develop trade, tourism and logistics opportunities, and accelerate the Syrian business world's access to international markets. Murathan Kalyoncu, Chairman of the Board of Kalyon Holding, stated that this investment will make significant contributions to Syria's sustainable development and that Turkish companies are happy to offer their experience in mega projects such as Istanbul Airport to serve Syria.

62nd International Damascus Fair opens in Syria

The opening of the 62nd International Damascus Fair, held in the Syrian capital Damascus after the fall of the Bashar al-Assad regime, was held with the participation of President Ahmed Shara.

Damascus Fair will open new cooperation doors in Turkey-Syria trade

Trade Minister Ömer Bolat stated that the 62nd Damascus International Fair, which will open its doors tomorrow in Syria, is of great importance in terms of reviewing the mutual cooperation of the two countries, especially taking international steps in the customs field.

Turkey-Syria Business Forum held in Aleppo

The Turkey-Syria Business Forum related to the construction and building materials sector was held in Aleppo, northern Syria.

Business world focuses on economic impacts of developments in Syria

Business world representatives expect the new era in Syria to create new opportunities for Turkey.

At least half of Syrians will return

After the fall of the Baath regime, Syrians began to return to their country. Syrian Businessmen Association President Halit Savafoğlu said, "We think that at least half of the Syrians living in Turkey will return. Those who see that their country has improved will go back. However, we cannot completely break our ties. We have a debt of gratitude to this country. We ate bread here. We are grateful to Turkey."

Syrian economy growing faster than expected, sanctions easing

Central Bank of Syria Governor Abdulkadir Husriye announced that with the return of refugees and the end of the 14-year civil war, the economy grew much faster in 2025 than the 1% growth rate predicted by the World Bank.
