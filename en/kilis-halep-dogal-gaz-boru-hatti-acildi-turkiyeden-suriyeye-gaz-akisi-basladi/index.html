Kilis-Aleppo natural gas pipeline opened: Gas flow from Turkey to Syria begins

The repair of the Kilis-Aleppo natural gas pipeline with a daily capacity of 6 million cubic meters of gas has been completed. With the opening of the Turkey-Syria Natural Gas Pipeline, the export of natural gas from Azerbaijan to Syria via Kilis began as of August 2, 2025.

At the historic ceremony held in Kilis, Energy and Natural Resources Minister Alparslan Bayraktar announced that in the first phase, 2 billion cubic meters of natural gas will be exported to Syria annually, and this will increase daily electricity supply in Syria from 3-4 hours to 10 hours. Turkey currently supplies electricity to Syria from 8 points; it was stated that with natural gas flow, this capacity will increase by 25% in a short time and reach 860 megawatts with new connections. In this way, approximately 1.6 million households in Syria will see improvement in their electricity. The project was implemented thanks to Turkey's cooperation with Azerbaijan and Qatar; a swap agreement was signed with SOCAR for Azerbaijani gas. Bayraktar emphasized that this step will alleviate the energy crisis in Syria and make a great contribution to the country's reconstruction efforts in terms of energy infrastructure, and could accelerate the return of Syrian refugees. It was also announced that a $7 billion strategic agreement was signed with a consortium consisting of Turkish, Qatari and US companies for the repair of infrastructure damaged in oil production and the establishment of new power plants, and that more than 5,000 MW of new electricity capacity will be created in Syria in the next 2-3 years.
