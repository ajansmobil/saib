Our Privacy Policy

As SAIB Association, when you visit our website and use our membership system, some information is collected through cookies to improve our services and enhance user experience. This information, except for your identity information, is used to better present our association activities and improve member experience. Cookies are used to remember your preferences, optimize the operation of our site, and provide you with a better experience.

The collected information is not shared with third parties and is used only for conducting association activities, announcing events, and providing better service to our members. Users can prevent the use of cookies by changing their browser settings. However, you should remember that in this case, some parts of our site may not work properly.

Your privacy and the protection of your personal data are extremely important to SAIB Association. We take the necessary technical and administrative measures to ensure the security of your personal information. If you have any questions or concerns about our Privacy Policy, you can contact our association headquarters.
