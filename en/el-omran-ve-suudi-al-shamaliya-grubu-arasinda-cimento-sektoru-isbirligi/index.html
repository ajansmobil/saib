Cement sector cooperation between "El Omran" and Saudi "Al-Shamaliya" group

Mahmud Fadile, General Manager of the Syrian General Cement and Building Materials Company "El Omran", explained the details of the cooperation agreement signed with the Saudi "Al-Shamaliya" Cement Group at the Syria-Saudi Investment Forum held in Damascus on July 24.

According to the statement made to SANA in Istanbul, this agreement focuses on increasing the capacity of technical personnel in Syria and strengthening technical cooperation between the two countries. Under the agreement signed by Al-Shamaliya Group CEO Ubeyd Al-Sebai on behalf of the Saudi side, Syrian technical personnel will be trained at the Adra Cement Factory Training and Rehabilitation Center near Damascus. The agreement also includes the application of occupational safety, occupational health, and environmental pollution reduction techniques, as well as modern production technologies. It was also decided to prepare a work plan for the maintenance and repair of cement equipment and to provide technical support in the production of special cement types not produced in the country. It was reported that this 90-day program, which can be extended if necessary, will be implemented immediately, and Saudi engineers and technicians will conduct the training program in Adra. This cooperation is considered an important step towards the Syrian Ministry of Industry's goal of developing the technical and production capacity of the cement sector. On the other hand, Saudi Arabia's Investment Minister Khalid Al-Falih announced that three new cement factories will be established in Syria with a total investment of 11 billion Saudi Riyals (approximately 3 billion USD). On July 23, Syrian Economy Minister Nidal Shaar and Minister Al-Falih laid the foundation together for the "Feyha" project, Syria's first white cement factory, in the Adra Industrial Zone in rural Damascus. These developments show that concrete industrial cooperation between Syria and Saudi Arabia has begun.
