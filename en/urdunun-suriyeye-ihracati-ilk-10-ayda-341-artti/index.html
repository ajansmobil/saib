Jordan's exports to Syria increased by 341% in the first 10 months

Jordan's national exports to Syria reached 203 million Jordanian dinars in the first 10 months of 2025, showing a 341.3% increase on an annual basis.

According to Jordan Statistics Department data, exports from Jordan to Syria rose to 203 million dinars (equivalent to 286 million dollars) in the January-October 2025 period. During the same period, Jordan's imports from Syria also increased by 47% to 75 million dinars. Thus, the total trade volume between the two countries increased from 97 million dinars in the same period of the previous year to 278 million dinars. This large increase is considered a reflection of Jordan's support for Syria's recovery and integration process. In May, the two countries agreed to prepare a cooperation roadmap focused on investment, joint ventures and reconstruction. The main items of Jordan's exports to Syria consist of construction materials such as cement, iron-steel, marble, ceramics, paint, pipes, as well as food, agricultural and chemical products. In addition, talks were held between the central banks of the two countries to strengthen cooperation in banking and finance. At a time when Jordan's general exports to Arab countries are also rising, the revival in trade relations with Syria contributes to regional economic cooperation.
